Built by a player, for the binder hunt

Binder Diver helps you find the singles you need in a game store's binders without hours of research.

What it does

Paste a decklist and Binder Diver looks up every printing of every card. It ranks the store binders most likely to hold them and walks you through the store one binder at a time.

How it picks binders

Binder Diver doesn't see any store's stock. It predicts. Common printings from big, recent, mainstream sets are the likeliest to be on hand, so those binders come first. When a binder doesn't have a card, Finish binder sends it to the next-best one.

Your binder groups teach it how your store files sets, and which sets it doesn't carry.

How a dive works

From decklist to cards in hand, in five steps.

  1. 1

    Paste your list

    Export a decklist from Moxfield, Archidekt or EDHREC and paste it. Binder Diver cleans up counts, set codes and typos, and asks about anything it can't match.

    [SCREENSHOT: import, fix names]
  2. 2

    Mark what you have

    Set cards you own, have ordered or are proxying. Everything left as Needed goes on your hunt.

    [SCREENSHOT: import step 4]
  3. 3

    Check your dive plan

    Every set your cards were printed in becomes a possible binder. Binder Diver ranks them, favoring common printings from big, recent sets that stores usually stock, and marks which are worth a pull.

    [SCREENSHOT: dive plan]
  4. 4

    Dive at the store

    Pull one binder at a time. Tap Found as you go. Finish binder sends anything you didn't find on to its next-best binder.

    [SCREENSHOT: store dive]
  5. 5

    Buy the rest

    When the trip ends, send what's left to TCGplayer in one tap, find another store nearby, or come back another day.

    [SCREENSHOT: trip end]

Questions

Does Binder Diver know what my store has in stock?

No. It predicts the most likely binders from where each card was printed. Tap Finish binder when you're done with one and the leftovers move on.

Do I need an account?

No. Your lists are saved in this browser. Accounts for syncing between devices come later.

My store keeps several sets in one binder.

Use Binder groups to tell Binder Diver, and mark sets your store doesn't carry.

Is it free?

Yes, always. Tips help keep it running.

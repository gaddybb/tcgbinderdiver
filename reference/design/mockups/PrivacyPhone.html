Privacy, in plain language

Last updated [DATE]. This page explains what Binder Diver keeps, what it shares, and your choices.

Stored on your device

Your lists, card statuses, binder groups and settings are saved in this browser. They aren't sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data for Binder Diver deletes them.

Analytics, only if you allow it

If you choose Allow analytics, Binder Diver uses Google Analytics to count things like which pages are visited and which features get used. It doesn't collect your name or email, and it isn't used to sell anything.

Card data

Card details and images come from Scryfall. Your browser asks Scryfall for them directly, so Scryfall sees those requests under its own privacy policy.

Store search

When you ask to find nearby stores, your approximate location is sent to Google Maps to show results. That only happens when you tap it.

Affiliate links

Buy on TCGplayer buttons are affiliate links through Impact. If you buy after tapping one, Binder Diver may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. TCGplayer may set its own cookies once you're on its site.

Accounts

There are no accounts yet. If they're added later, this page will explain what's stored and how to delete it.

Step 1 · No card names found

Replaces the summary on step 1 when no line looks like a card.

We couldn't read any card names

Binder Diver looks for one card per line, with an optional count in front. These all work:

Sol Ring
1 Sol Ring
1x Sol Ring (CMM) 400 *F*

Tip: in Moxfield, use Export, then Copy as plain text.

Step 1 · Too many cards

Replaces the summary on step 1 when the paste is over 250 cards.

This paste has 412 cards

A list can hold up to 250 cards. Bigger collections will import in a later version.
